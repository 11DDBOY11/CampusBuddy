About KCET Admission
The Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) is conducted by the
Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA)
for admission to Engineering, Architecture, Pharmacy, and other professional courses in Karnataka. Admissions to AIET through KCET are governed by the rules and regulations laid down by KEA and the Government of Karnataka.
Step-by-Step Admission Process
01
Register for KCET
Candidates must appear for the Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA). Register online at the official KEA website.
02
Obtain KCET Rank
After the examination, collect your KCET rank card from the KEA portal. The rank will determine your eligibility for seat allotment in various colleges.
03
Participate in Online Counseling
Register for KEA online counseling. Submit your college and branch preferences in order of priority. Participate in document verification at the designated Help Line Centre (HLC).
04
Seat Allotment
Seats are allotted based on KCET rank, category, and preferences submitted. Check the allotment result on the KEA portal and download your Seat Allotment Order.
05
Report to AIET
Report to Alva's Institute of Engineering & Technology with all original documents and the allotment order within the stipulated time. Pay the required fees to confirm admission.
Eligibility Criteria
Passed 2nd PUC / 12th Standard with English as one of the Languages
Minimum 45% aggregate in Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry / Bio-Technology / Biology / Electronics / Computer Science
For Karnataka SC, ST, and OBC candidates — minimum aggregate is 40%
Must have appeared and qualified in KCET conducted by KEA
Karnataka domicile or eligible NRI / PIO candidates as per KEA norms
