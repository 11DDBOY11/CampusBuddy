Welcome to the
Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
at
Alva's Institute of Engineering and Technology (AIET)
—a distinguished center of academic excellence committed to innovation, sustainability, and real-world problem solving. The department strives to transform knowledge into practice, nurturing future-ready technology professionals equipped to meet the dynamic demands of the global industry.
It is a matter of pride that the department has successfully completed nearly
two decades
of impactful academic excellence at AIET.
Specializations
The department offers specialization across five focused streams of Computer Science and Engineering:
Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
Cyber Security
UI/UX Design with iOS
Data Science
Computational Complexity
Accreditation
The
B.E. (Computer Science and Engineering)
programme at AIET has been accredited by the
National Board of Accreditation (NBA)
for the period
2019–2028
, reflecting the department's sustained commitment to quality and excellence in engineering education.
